Quick Installation

How to Install AuroraVPN APK on Android

Takes less than 30 seconds. No root, no Google account required.

1

Download APK

Tap the Download APK button above to save AuroraVPN.apk onto your Android device or phone.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

Tap the downloaded notification. If Android asks, tap Settings and toggle "Allow from this source".

3

Tap Install

Confirm installation. Android will install AuroraVPN directly into your app drawer.

4

Tap Connect

Open the app, choose a server, tap Connect, and click OK when Android displays the standard VPN permission prompt.

Android VpnService

Uses official Linux TUN interface allocation via VpnService.Builder(). Encapsulates all IPv4/IPv6 socket descriptors through protect(socket) to prevent routing loops.
